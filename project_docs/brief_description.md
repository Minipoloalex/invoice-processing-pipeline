# Portline Logistics

Portline Logistics is a mid-size freight forwarding company headquartered in Lisbon. They handle shipping coordination, customs brokerage, and supply chain logistics for clients across Europe and the United States.

The company works with a diverse set of vendors — technology providers, consulting firms, infrastructure partners — across multiple countries. Their vendor base includes companies in Portugal, Germany, the US, and other markets, each with their own invoicing formats, currencies, and tax conventions.

Portline has been growing steadily, and what used to be a manageable finance workload is becoming a bottleneck.
## The Problem

Portline's finance team processes approximately **200 invoices per month**. Today, the process is entirely manual:

- Invoice PDFs arrive from vendors via email or the ERP
- A finance analyst opens each PDF and reads through it
- They manually extract the key data — vendor name, tax ID, amounts, line items, dates
- They cross-reference the vendor against company records to verify the details match
- They flag any discrepancies — wrong amounts, unknown vendors, missing tax IDs
- Everything gets typed into a spreadsheet for review

This process is **slow** (each invoice takes 5–10 minutes), **error-prone** (manual data entry mistakes are common), and **frustrating** for the team. As invoice volume grows, the team can't keep up.

The invoices themselves are messy. Different vendors use different formats. Some invoices include tax breakdowns, some don't. Some are in different currencies. Some reference previous invoices (credit notes). Vendor names don't always match exactly what's in the company records.

The finance team needs a way to **automate the extraction and validation** and get a clear view of what's been processed, what looks correct, and what needs their attention.
